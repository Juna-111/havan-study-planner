# Student quick guide

## 1. Sign in

Open Havan and sign in. New students create an account first.

## 2. Set up your profile

Choose your university, curriculum, stream, courses, and study availability. If you do not choose a current chapter or topic position, Havan starts from the beginning.

## 3. Choose what to study

Open **Plan → Build a plan**. Select the topics you actually want to study and choose the time window.

## 4. Review the plan

Havan organizes the selected topics around your available time and exams. Read the reason shown for each task. Topics that do not fit are shown rather than silently disappearing.

## 5. Follow the plan

Use **Home** for the immediate view and **Plan** for the full schedule. Start, complete, skip, move, repeat, or remove tasks as needed.

## 6. Progress

Open **Progress** to see completed topics.

## 7. Settings

Open **Settings** for account information and study preferences.

Support principle: Havan recommends. The student remains in control.
